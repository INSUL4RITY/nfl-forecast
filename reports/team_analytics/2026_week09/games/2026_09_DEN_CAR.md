## Mismatches (evidence, mechanism, neutralisers)
- **Weak protection vs elite pass rush** (CAR offence vs DEN defence), favours DEN, confidence moderate: CAR allows pressure on 27.4% (32nd in protection); DEN generates 23.6% (5th). Mechanism: Pressure that shortens dropbacks, creates sacks and forces hurried throws on obvious passing downs. Neutralisers: PFR pressure data is missing 13 games.
- **Four-man pressure vs poor protection** (CAR offence vs DEN defence), favours DEN, confidence moderate: DEN hits or sacks the quarterback on 19.5% of dropbacks without blitzing (1st); CAR allows pressure on 27.4% (32nd in protection). Mechanism: Pressure without extra rushers, so the defence keeps seven in coverage and the quarterback has fewer open targets. Neutralisers: Hit-or-sack is a play-level proxy for pressure; free data has no play-level pressure.
- **Poor tackling vs yards after contact and catch** (CAR offence vs DEN defence), favours CAR, confidence limited: DEN misses 10.9% of tackle attempts (31st) and allows 2.47 rushing yards after contact (32nd); CAR gains 1.86 yards after contact per carry (7th) and 0.31 YAC over expected per catch (8th). Mechanism: Short throws and runs that turn into chunk gains after the first contact. Neutralisers: PFR tackling data is missing 13 games.
