## Mismatches (evidence, mechanism, neutralisers)
- **Explosive passing vs deep-ball vulnerability** (NE offence vs LAC defence), favours NE, confidence limited: NE completes 20+ yard passes on 11.4% of attempts (5th); LAC allows 9.3% (17th) and +0.945 EPA on throws of 20+ air yards (29th, 29 faced). Mechanism: Shot plays off play-action and vertical routes against the coverage's weakest depth. Matchup-specific: NE attempts 45 deep passes (+0.462 EPA each). Neutralisers: Explosive rates are the noisiest passing measure (shrinkage keeps about a third of each team's record).
- **Four-man pressure vs poor protection** (NE offence vs LAC defence), favours LAC, confidence limited: LAC hits or sacks the quarterback on 14.3% of dropbacks without blitzing (8th); NE allows pressure on 22.3% (22nd in protection). Mechanism: Pressure without extra rushers, so the defence keeps seven in coverage and the quarterback has fewer open targets. Neutralisers: Hit-or-sack is a play-level proxy for pressure; free data has no play-level pressure.
- **Poor tackling vs yards after contact and catch** (LAC offence vs NE defence), favours LAC, confidence limited: NE misses 8.4% of tackle attempts (18th) and allows 1.96 rushing yards after contact (26th); LAC gains 1.88 yards after contact per carry (7th) and -0.54 YAC over expected per catch (24th). Mechanism: Short throws and runs that turn into chunk gains after the first contact. Neutralisers: PFR tackling data is missing 1 games.
