## Mismatches (evidence, mechanism, neutralisers)
- **Passing offence vs pass defence** (PIT offence vs DEN defence), favours DEN, confidence moderate: PIT passing 24th (adj. EPA/dropback -0.068, 6.20 Y/A); DEN pass defence 5th (allows -0.058, 6.71 Y/A). Mechanism: Coverage that limits completions and forces the offence to sustain long drives without big gains.
- **Strong protection vs weak pass rush** (DEN offence vs PIT defence), favours DEN, confidence limited: DEN allows pressure on 18.9% of dropbacks (7th); PIT generates 17.4% (30th). Mechanism: Clean pockets: more time for intermediate and deep concepts, fewer sacks and fewer drive-killing negative plays. Neutralisers: PIT may blitz more to compensate (blitz rate 28.3%).
- **Red-zone offence vs red-zone defence** (PIT offence vs DEN defence), favours DEN, confidence limited: PIT scores touchdowns on 44.8% of red-zone trips (29th, 29 trips); DEN allows 51.5% (7th, 33 trips). Mechanism: Converting trips into touchdowns rather than field goals: a four-point swing per trip. Neutralisers: Offensive red-zone TD rate has no reliable team-to-team signal yet; it regresses strongly towards the league average.
- **Poor tackling vs yards after contact and catch** (PIT offence vs DEN defence), favours PIT, confidence limited: DEN misses 11.0% of tackle attempts (31st) and allows 2.31 rushing yards after contact (32nd); PIT gains 2.23 yards after contact per carry (2nd) and 0.32 YAC over expected per catch (8th). Mechanism: Short throws and runs that turn into chunk gains after the first contact. Neutralisers: PFR tackling data is missing 1 games.
