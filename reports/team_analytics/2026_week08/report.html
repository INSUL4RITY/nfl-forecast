Week 8 takeaways

Statistical assessments built from play-by-play efficiency, not from records or market expectations. Seven weeks is a real sample but a small one: treat every ranking below as an estimate with wide error bars.

1

Data, validation and method

Window: 2026 regular season, weeks 1-7 (107 games, 13,805 scrimmage plays, 2,188 drives). Generated 2026-10-27T16:49:26+00:00. Historical seasons 2021-2025 are used only for reference models and weights, never as 2026 evidence.

Plays and filters

  • Scrimmage play: nflfastR pass or rush flag, EPA present, down present (two-point tries excluded), kneel-downs and spikes excluded. Plays nullified by penalty that nflfastR tags as a pass or rush are included, as in nflfastR's standard team summaries.
  • Dropback = pass attempts, sacks and scrambles. Designed rush = nflfastR rush (scrambles excluded). QB designed runs are designed rushes by players with 5+ dropbacks; they are reported separately from scrambles.
  • Success = EPA > 0. Early downs = 1st and 2nd. Late downs = third- and fourth-down conversion flags. Red zone = inside the opponent's 20.
  • Explosive: completed or attempted pass (not a scramble or sack) gaining 20+ yards; designed rush or scramble gaining 10+ yards; live plays only.
  • WP 10-90% columns drop plays when either side's win probability is outside 10-90%, as a garbage-time check.
  • Hit-or-sack rate uses the pbp qb_hit and sack flags. It is a proxy, not pressure: free data has no play-level pressure.

Opponent adjustment

For each metric (EPA/play, success, EPA/dropback, EPA/designed rush, early-down EPA) a play-level least-squares fit y = intercept + home + offence(team) + defence(team) with sum-to-zero team effects and no shrinkage. The adjusted value is the league mean plus the team effect, so a raw and an adjusted value sit on the same scale. Schedule strength is the play-weighted average opponent effect. The home coefficient this season is -0.011 EPA/play. Quarterback adjustment subtracts the average dropback-defence effect of the defences each passer faced.

Composite rankings and weights

EPA/play, success rate and points per drive measure overlapping things, so the composites are weighted by how well each one, measured over weeks 1-7, predicted a team's opponent-adjusted EPA/play over the rest of the season in 2021-2025 (non-negative least squares on within-season z-scores, 160 team-seasons). The regression handles their correlation, so nothing is double-counted.

Seven-week defensive numbers explain only about a tenth of rest-of-season defensive performance (R² 0.11), against about a third for offence (0.37). Read defensive rankings with more caution.

Overall team rating = (adj. offensive EPA/play − league mean) × 64.5 plays + (league mean − adj. defensive EPA/play) × 64.5 + special-teams EPA per game, in points per game. EPA is in points, so this needs no arbitrary weights. Special teams is kept separate everywhere else. Situational = equal-weight z-scores of late-down conversion and red-zone TD rate (two distinct situations). Passing, rushing, pass D, run D rank on one adjusted metric each.

Prior-season stabilisation

The "with 2025 prior" rating shrinks each 2026 adjusted EPA/play towards a prior of league mean + b × (team's 2025 adjusted value − 2025 mean). Year-to-year slopes from 2021-2025: offence b = 0.41, defence b = 0.20. The weight on 2026 is τ² / (τ² + σ²/n), with τ² the variance of true change between seasons (offence 0.0049, defence 0.0022) and σ²/n the sampling variance of this season's estimate. On average the 2026 data gets 54% of the weight on offence and 35% on defence, and the share rises each week. Teams with a new primary quarterback are flagged, because last season's offence then says less.

Scoring and drives

Drives are nflfastR fixed_drive. Points per drive are the offence's score change from the start of the drive to the start of the next drive, so extra points count and defensive or return scores do not. Every game's drive points reconcile to its final score. Points over start EP = drive points minus nflfastR's expected points at the first snap. That measures finishing relative to field position (it is a next-score expectation, so it is approximate).

Expected wins, luck and turnovers

  • Pythagorean expected wins use exponent 2.37.
  • EPA-based expected wins: P(win) = logistic(0.596 × game net EPA), fitted on 2021-2025 games, summed over games played.
  • Luck index = mean of (wins − Pythagorean xW) and (wins − EPA xW). At +0.75 or more a team is a potential overperformer, at −0.75 or less a potential underperformer. With 6-7 games the sampling error of either difference is about one win.
  • Expected interceptions: each charted attempt gets the league rate for its FTN interception-worthy status (worthy 56.2%, not worthy 0.34%). Expected fumbles lost: own fumbles × 0.486, the 2021-25 loss rate.

Fourth downs

A transparent reference, not nfl4th. For 4th downs with win probability 20-80% and more than two minutes left in the half, each option (go, punt, field goal) is valued at the average expected points after that choice in the same distance × field-position bucket in 2021-25 (buckets with 20+ cases). Selection bias (teams go when conditions favour it) and ignoring the clock limit it.

Penalties

nflfastR records one penalty per play, so no play is counted twice. Declined and offsetting penalties are excluded from the counts. Phase: special teams if the play was a kick, otherwise offence or defence by the penalised team. The EPA cost counts no-play penalties only; for accepted penalties on live plays the penalty and the play cannot be separated. Negated explosive plays and TDs are parsed from the play text.

Not available, and proxies used

  • DVOA: FTN's proprietary metric. It is not in nflverse and was not used, so every DVOA column reads n/a. The closest computed analogues here are opponent-adjusted EPA and success rate. They are not DVOA.
  • Coverage (man/zone), time to pressure, pressure by play: not in free data. PFR pressures are game-level, so pressure-when-blitzing uses the hit-or-sack proxy.
  • Pressure coverage: PFR advanced stats are available for 94 of 107 games; pressure rates use only those games.
  • FTN charting is missing one game (week 7 DAL at PHI), so blitz and expected-INT figures for those two teams have one fewer game.
  • Participation data (players on field per play) is not published for 2026, so personnel groupings and on/off splits are not possible. Availability uses snap counts, weekly rosters and injury reports.
  • Week 8 injury reports are not out yet. Statuses come from each team's latest report (week 7, or week 6 after a bye) and the week 8 roster list.
  • Timeout use is not scored: there is no defensible free benchmark. Bye effects rest on one game per team.
  • Blitz: FTN's blitz is five or more rushers, so "blitz" and "5+ rushers" are the same split here.

Quality checks

  • Schedule lists 107 regular-season games in weeks 1-7; play-by-play contains 107; 107 have final scores.
  • Opponent adjustment: home-field coefficient -0.011 EPA/play for the offence (half the home/away gap); play-level residual variance 1.820.
  • Drive points reconcile with final scores in 107 of 107 games (max gap 0 pts; gaps come from scores on plays outside a scrimmage drive).
  • Drives: 2298 built, 110 excluded (kneel-only, or started with 30 s or less left in a half).
  • FTN charting: 99.1% of 2026 pass attempts charted (missing: 2026_07_DAL_PHI). League P(INT | interception-worthy) = 0.562; P(INT | not) = 0.0034. Historical own-fumble loss rate 0.486 (2021-2025).
  • PFR advanced passing: 188 of 214 team-games available (94 games); pressure and PFR-blitz rates use only those games.

Power rankings

Ten rankings, each built from one clearly defined measure or from a composite with fitted weights (see Method). Click a column header to rank by it.

3

Advanced efficiency: EPA, success rate and DVOA

EPA and DVOA are different measures. EPA (expected points added) values each play by the change in nflfastR's expected points from before to after the snap, from down, distance, field position and time. It is open, play-by-play and unadjusted unless an adjustment is applied. DVOA (FTN) compares each play's success with a league baseline for the same situation, then adjusts for opponent and removes some luck-driven outcomes (for example, fumble recovery is treated as random). DVOA is proprietary and is not used here: every DVOA cell reads n/a. The opponent-adjusted columns below are EPA adjusted by a published regression (Method), not DVOA.
4

Points per drive and scoring efficiency

Per-drive rates remove differences in the number of possessions. Points over start EP measures what a unit did with the field position it was given.

5

Passing offence and pass defence

Quarterback and protection are separated where the data allows: hit/sack versus clean-pocket EPA, sacks per pressure (partly the quarterback), drops and YAC over expected (receivers). Man and zone coverage splits are not available in free data.

6

Rushing offence and run defence

Designed runs, quarterback designed runs and scrambles are kept apart. Yards before contact points to blocking; yards after contact and rush yards over expected point to the ball carrier. Both are imperfect splits.

Penalties: offensive and defensive discipline

Counts are accepted penalties. Pre-snap fouls are the most clearly controllable. Pass interference, holding and illegal contact also come with aggressive coverage, and some calls are judgement calls by the officials, so a high count is not automatically a lack of discipline.

9

Turnovers, margin and sustainability

Expected values use FTN's interception-worthy charting and fumble counts. Interceptions that are dropped, tipped or caused by receiver error remain noise the charting only partly captures. A large gap between actual and expected margin suggests regression is possible, not certain.

10

Luck, close games and regression candidates

Close-game records are not treated as luck on their own: quarterback quality, coaching and late-game execution all affect them. The categories are in section C.

11

Blitzing, pressure and pass protection

Three different things. Blitz rate is how often a defence sends five or more rushers (FTN charting). Pressure rate is how often the quarterback is hurried, hit or sacked (PFR, game totals). Sack rate is sacks per dropback. A defence can blitz a lot without pressure, or create pressure with four and keep seven in coverage.
12

Quarterback evaluation and supporting cast

Per-quarterback, not per-team: a team's totals can mix several passers. Composite = 50% opponent-adjusted EPA/dropback, 30% CPOE, 20% interception-worthy rate (lower is better). This weighting is a stated judgement, not a fitted model. Read it beside the individual columns.

13

Strength of schedule and quality of opposition

Opponent strength is the opponents' opponent-adjusted net EPA per play, not their records, which avoids circular reasoning. Tiers are league thirds of that rating. Results against tiers rest on two or three games each.

14

Coaching, game management and situational decisions

Observed decisions, not inferred coaching quality. A fourth-down attempt that failed can still be the right call. The reference compares each decision with historical average outcomes, not with a win-probability model.

Depth, injuries and personnel

Confirmed absences (reserve list, or Out/Doubtful) are kept apart from Questionable and unexplained absences. Absences are weighted by snap share, not by an invented point value. The week 8 injury reports were not yet published.
