# Model audit, November 2026 (Phase 1: audit only)

Written 2026-11-19. Nothing in the model, its weights, the data or the website was changed. Every number below comes
from a script in `scripts/audit_2026_11/` (rerun with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\audit_2026_11\<script>.py`, in
the order s1, s2, s3, s4, s4b, s5). Outputs are in `reports/audit_2026_11/` (one JSON per script, plus prediction
files).

**Ground rules used:**
- **Walk-forward 2019–2024:** train on 2016..S−1, predict S, at the final horizon (60 min before kickoff), with the
  ridge alpha chosen chronologically inside each fold, exactly as `src/nflcast/evaluation/backtest.py` does.
- **2025 is never a test season.** The 2026 extra check trains on 2016–2024 (2025 left out, as asked) and covers the
  118 finished games of weeks 3–10.
- **The model audited is the published "Model line":** the football-only ridge, feature set `core_qb`,
  `src/nflcast/models/core.py:123`.
- **Cross-check:** my baseline reproduces the existing backtest (`reports/backtest/LATEST.md`, B_qb, final horizon):
  margin MAE 10.41 / RMSE 13.35 for 2019–21 (report: 10.41 / 13.34) and 9.75 / 12.72 for 2022–24 (report: 9.75 / 12.72).

**Decision rule (from the brief, fixed in `scripts/audit_2026_11/common.py` before any experiment ran).** A change counts
as an improvement only if both hold:
- (a) the paired per-game difference in absolute margin error has a 95% season-week block-bootstrap CI below zero over
  2019–24 (1,675 games, 130 weeks, 2,000 resamples);
- (b) it improves margin MAE in at least 4 of the 6 seasons.

Anything else is "no demonstrated improvement". Positive differences mean the candidate is worse.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Home field and neutral sites (`s1_venue.py` → `reports/audit_2026_11/s1_venue.json`)

**How home field is built.**
- **One league-wide coefficient, no team-specific terms:** the `venue` context input is +1 for the home team's row,
  −1 for the away team's, and 0 at neutral sites (`src/nflcast/models/core.py:145`).
- **Fitted on every training season:** the frozen artifact covers 2016 to 2026 week 2 (2,793 games), 2020 included.
- **Its value:** **+1.88 points of margin** in the frozen Model line. In the walk-forward folds it is +1.67, +1.39,
  +1.22, +1.28, +1.40 and +1.55 for 2019–2024 (ridge-shrunk, alpha chosen per fold).
- **Same convention in the ratings:** the opponent-adjusted ratings have their own venue term with the same rule
  (`src/nflcast/features/asof.py:73`).

**Neutral sites are handled correctly in the model.**
- **The flag:** `neutral_site` = nflverse `location == "Neutral"` (`src/nflcast/data/games.py:41`). It gives zero home
  field in the model features, the opponent-adjusted ratings and the naive baseline (`core.py` `NaiveHome`).
- **The two 2026 cases:**
  - 2026_04_IND_WAS (Tottenham Hotspur Stadium) and 2026_06_HOU_JAX (Wembley) are both `neutral_site = true`.
  - Their releases show "Home field 0.0" (`releases/2026/week_04/rel_20261004T131557Z.json`,
    `week_06/rel_20261018T124607Z.json`).
  - All 9 international games of 2026 are flagged neutral, as are all 34 international games of 2016–2024.
- **What the brief noticed is a display bug, not a model bug.** The matchup game log labels a neutral-site game by
  nflverse's designated home team (`src/nflcast/predict/matchup.py:113-115`, `web/components/MatchupDetail.tsx:92`).
  So IND's London game appears as "@ WAS" (`web/public/data/weeks/2026-05.json`, IND log, week 4: `"home": false`).
- **No ratings or historical errors to re-measure,** since the model is not affected.

**Empirical home margin** (non-neutral games; mean ± SE):

| Seasons | Home margin |
|---|---|
| 2016–18 | +3.06, +2.64, +2.32 |
| 2019 | +0.09 (±0.91) |
| 2020 (no crowds) | +0.17 (±0.87) |
| 2021–24 | +1.81, +2.25, +2.95, +2.03 |
| 2026 to date | **−0.28 ± 1.13** (142 games) |

**Home-field variants** (1,675 games):

| Variant | MAE (current 10.075) | Diff [95% CI] | Seasons better | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No home field | 10.142 | +0.067 [+0.005, +0.133] | 1/6 | worse |
| Constant from the last 3 training seasons* | 10.129 | +0.054 [+0.022, +0.088] | 1/6 | worse |
| Team-specific, empirical-Bayes shrunk | 10.087 | +0.012 [−0.003, +0.029] | 3/6 | no demonstrated improvement |

\* The brief asked for "post-2020 only". Folds 2019–2021 have no post-2020 training data, so the last 3 training seasons
were used instead.

- **Team-specific spread is small:** the between-team SD of home field is 0.25–1.02 points, and the largest shrunk team
  deviation is 1.3 points.
- **2026 check (118 games):** current 9.10, no home field **8.74**, recent constant 9.23, team-specific 9.13. That fits
  the low 2026 home margin, but it is one partial season against six full ones. It is a reason to watch, not to change.
- **Neutral-site games in the backtest:** 34, MAE 9.40 (too few to judge).

### 2.2 Prediction intervals and win probabilities (`s2_distributions.py` → `s2_distributions.json`)

**Method (verified).**
- **Intervals:** forecast + empirical quantiles of the same model's **out-of-fold** residuals from earlier seasons.
- **Win probability:** logistic on the out-of-fold predicted margin.
- **Production:** fits both on the walk-forward 2019–2025 out-of-fold predictions of the locked-test run
  (`src/nflcast/predict/release.py:216-224`, intervals `:443-448`). That is out of sample for every 2026 game.
- **This audit refitted them per test season** on 2017..S−1 only (folds 2017–18 exist only to calibrate 2019).
- **Levels:** the site publishes 80% and 95% (`configs/settings.yaml:36`). The 50% level was audited but is not shown.
- **One approximation:** production intervals mix the QB scenarios; the historical backtest uses the actual starter as
  a proxy, so this checks the method, not the exact published widths.

**Coverage, pooled 2019–24** (1,675 games). Below/above = share of results outside the interval on each side.

| Model | Target | 50% | 80% | 95% | 80% width |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model line (B_qb) | margin | 0.510 | 0.796 (0.095 / 0.109) | 0.955 | 33.2 pts |
| Model line | total | 0.523 | **0.837** (0.074 / 0.089) | 0.959 | 36.5 |
| Market-adjusted (C_resid_noinj) | margin | **0.533** | 0.810 | 0.959 | 33.4 |
| Market-adjusted | total | **0.530** | **0.826** | **0.961** | 34.5 |

Bold = misses its target by more than 1.96 binomial SEs.

- **Margins are well calibrated.**
- **Totals intervals are slightly too wide:** the 80% interval covers 83–84%. Older, higher-variance scoring seasons are
  in the residual pool.
  - The existing backtest, which calibrates only on 2019 onward, shows less of this: 81.7%
    (`reports/backtest/LATEST.md`).

**Subgroup flags** (80% margin unless stated; many buckets were tested, so some flags are expected by chance):

| Subgroup | Games | Coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lines of 10.5+ points, Model line | 175 | **0.731** | 18.3% of results above the interval: lopsided games spread out more than the pooled residuals assume. Market-adjusted: 0.749 (not flagged). |
| Lines 3.5–7, market-adjusted | 656 | 0.840 | flagged (too wide) |
| Weeks 1–4 | 382 | 0.812 / 0.825 | fine for margins; totals over-cover (0.864 / 0.840) |
| QB-change games | 382 | 0.798 / 0.832 | fine |
| Neutral sites | 34 | 0.82 / 0.79 | too few to judge |
| By season, 80% margin | ~270–285 each | 0.765–0.863 | 2022 over-covers (0.849 / 0.863, flagged) |

**Win probabilities.**

| | Log loss | Brier (home win) | Range by season |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model line | 0.6472 | 0.2194 | log loss 0.617–0.669 |
| Market-adjusted | 0.6321 | 0.2126 | log loss 0.597–0.652 |

- **Reliability (Model line):** the 0.5–0.8 bins match observed rates closely: 0.551→0.551 (n=408), 0.652→0.672 (326),
  0.742→0.720 (225).
- **The thin tails under-state confidence:** 0.835→0.923 (78), 0.163→0.375 (24).

**Symmetry.**
- **Intervals are not forced to be symmetric:** they are the empirical quantiles of the pooled residuals.
- **From the favourite's side, the Model line slightly under-predicts favourites:** median residual +0.61
  [+0.01, +1.15] over all games. For predicted margins of 10+ it is +2.55 [−1.26, +4.74] (107 games), which is not
  significant. This fits ridge shrinkage and the lopsided-game under-coverage above.
- **Market-adjusted line:** no asymmetry (median −0.36 [−0.85, +0.07]).

### 2.4 Roster-aware ratings (`s4_roster.py`, `s4b_adjust.py` → `s4_roster.json`, `s4b_adjust.json`)

**Available player-level data** (all nflverse, archived locally):

| Data | Seasons |
|---|---|
| Snap counts by player-game (PFR) | 2012+ |
| Weekly rosters with status (active, reserve, suspended, inactive) | 2016+ |
| Injury reports | 2009+ (only the final weekly version survives historically) |
| Depth charts | 2016+ |
| Participation | 2016, 2019, 2021–25 |

There is **no transactions feed**. Releases and trades show up only as a player leaving the weekly roster. Since
2026-09-25 this project archives every snapshot as it is observed, so mid-week versions exist only from then.

**What the model does beyond QBs: nothing.**
- **The official Model line** has only QB inputs (`core.py:92`, `QB_TEAM`).
- **Non-QB injury inputs** exist as `core_qb_inj` (`core.py:94`), but were not promoted.
- **They are in the v2 trial line,** which is display only.
- **Reserve lists and roster moves** are display only (`predict/reserve_list.py`, `predict/roster_moves.py`).

**Size of the problem.**

*Definitions:*
- **Regular:** a non-QB, non-specialist player who took ≥60% of his unit's snaps in ≥2 earlier games this season.
- **Absent:** took no snaps in this game. Measured after the game, so this is a diagnostic only.

*Frequency:*
- Over 2019–24 (weeks 3+, 2,814 team-games) teams average 20.2 regulars, with **3.75 absent**.
- 34% of absences are new (the player played the previous game).
- 67% were knowable before kickoff: Out/Doubtful on the final report (25%), or on a reserve list / off the team in the
  latest earlier weekly roster (42%).
- That roster check wrongly flags 0.4–0.6% of players who did play.

**The bias is real but small** (team points, actual minus predicted; per one absent full-time starter; block-bootstrap
CI):

| Absences | Effect on team points | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Own offence | −0.37 | [−0.64, −0.10] |
| Opponent's defence | +0.65 | [+0.36, +0.92] |
| Weeks 3–16 only (excludes week-17/18 rest games), own offence | −0.35 | [−0.68, −0.06] |
| Weeks 3–16 only, opponent's defence | +0.54 | [+0.17, +0.88] |
| New absences, offence | −0.61 | [−1.07, −0.21] |
| Continuing absences, offence | −0.27 | [−0.61, +0.09] |
| Knowable-before-kickoff, offence | −0.42 | [−0.77, −0.05] |
| Knowable-before-kickoff, opponent's defence | +0.81 | [+0.48, +1.12] |

- **New absences matter most** because the form numbers have not caught up yet.
- **In practice:** teams missing ≥1.2 starter-shares on offence average −0.31 ± 0.24 pts against the prediction (1,448
  team-games); teams missing none, +0.60 ± 0.40 (571).

**Adjustment tested** (walk-forward, decision rule; snap-weighted known absences of regulars as two new inputs):

### 2.5 Game page and wording (assessment only)

- **Market marker on the total bar** of "Where the model line comes from" (`web/components/MarginBreakdown.tsx`):
  feasible.
  - Releases store the line used for the pick (`locked_pick.line_home_spread`; the latest line while provisional).
  - It should use the same line as the pick logic: the locked line after the lock, otherwise the forecast's line.
- **Home field as a starting row:** valid, because item 1 confirms it is a constant.
  - It is +1.88 for the home team and 0 at neutral sites, identical for every game.
  - Example: "Home field +1.9, matchup factors +5.8, model line HOU −7.7".
- **Linking contribution bars to "Unit against unit"** (`src/nflcast/predict/matchup.py:38-47`):

| Mapping | Contribution groups → unit rows |
|---|---|
| Clean | passing → passing efficiency, protection vs pass rush (sacks), explosive (pass part); rushing → rushing efficiency; scoring → red zone, points per drive; efficiency → play success |
| Partial | turnovers and field position: no unit row; pace: the neutral-situation style rows |
| None | QB (game log names starters), home field, rest, roof, other |

- **"Edge" wording.** Every place the site says "edge" or implies value (`s5_gap_evidence.py` output):
  - **Pick box tiers:** "Tiny edge · about a coin flip / Small edge / Moderate edge / Large edge"
    (`web/components/Pick.tsx:39`), plus the explainer text (`Pick.tsx:30`).
  - **Method page:** "the size of the edge …" (`web/app/methodology/page.tsx:254`) and "not a reliable edge" (`:257`).
  - **Performance page:** the line-movement column header "Avg value" (`web/components/LineMovement.tsx:30`).
  - **"X edge" labels in the matchup units** (`MatchupDetail.tsx:76`, Method `:168`) mean a league-rank advantage
    on the field, not betting value. They are fine as they are.

**Historical evidence for the tiers** (Model line, 2019–24, share of games finishing on the model's side of the
~closing line; level results excluded; Wilson 95% CI):

| Tier | Games | Model side |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny (<0.5) | 248 | 48.0% [41.8, 54.2] |
| Small (0.5–1.5) | 495 | 51.3% [46.9, 55.7] |
| Moderate (1.5–3) | 500 | 47.6% [43.3, 52.0] |
| Large 3–4 | 202 | 57.9% [51.0, 64.5] |
| Large 4+ | 188 | 51.1% [44.0, 58.1] |
| All gaps ≥ 3 | 390 | 54.6% [49.7, 59.5] |

- **No tier is reliably above 50%,** and the pattern is not monotonic. The one interval that excludes 50% sits between
  two that do not, which is what chance looks like with six tiers.
- **Proposed wording:** keep "Model pick", and say "gap" for the distance:
  - **"Small gap / Moderate gap / Large gap"** (a "Tiny gap" tier keeps "about a coin flip").
  - **Explainer:** "Gap: how far the model line is from the market line. In past seasons, larger gaps did not finish on
    the model's side more often than chance."
  - **"Avg value" → "Avg change in chance".**
  - **Keep the three ideas separate in the text:**
    - Model disagreement = gap.
    - Prediction accuracy = MAE and calibration (Performance).
    - Results vs the market line = the record, always with its game count and CI.

## 3. Bugs found (separate from model-improvement ideas)

1. **Neutral-site games shown as away/home in the matchup game log** (display only; the model is correct).
   - `src/nflcast/predict/matchup.py:113-115` sets `is_home` from nflverse's designated home team, and
     `web/components/MatchupDetail.tsx:92` prints "vs"/"@".
   - Example: IND's London game appears as "@ WAS".
   - Fix: carry `neutral_site` into the log and print "vs … (neutral)" or "· London".
2. **nflverse stadium names for 2025 international games are wrong** (data quality, upstream).
   - Seven 2025 games flagged `location = Neutral` carry the nominal home team's US stadium (e.g. 2025_01_KC_LAC at
     "SoFi Stadium", actually São Paulo; 2025_10_ATL_IND at "Lucas Oil Stadium", actually Berlin).
   - The neutral flag is right, so the model is unaffected.
   - Anything keyed on stadium name (venue time zone lookup `predict/export_web.py:55`, roof shares by stadium) would
     be wrong for those games. They are all historical; 2026 names are correct.
3. **Weekly rosters' `pfr_id` is missing for 36% of rows** (2023). Any join of snap counts (PFR ids) to rosters by
   `pfr_id` silently loses a third of players; the players table maps PFR to GSIS ids completely. Production code maps through the players
   table (`src/nflcast/features/personnel.py:202`); only this audit's first draft used the roster column, and was corrected.

## 4. Data limitations and methodological risks

- **Historical market lines** are one untimed value per game (≈ closing). Picks lock 24 h before kickoff, so the tier
  evidence above (closing line) is not the line picks are graded on.
- **The final-horizon backtest uses the actual starting QB as a proxy.** Published intervals mix QB scenarios, so live
  widths differ slightly.
- **Only the final weekly injury report survives historically;** mid-week versions exist only from 2026-09-25 (this
  project's archive).
